# ORGANIZATION

*How Anonymous Art is structured: the movement, the company, the works, and the people.*

There are two things called "AA." They are related but not the same, and conflating them is the main source of ambiguity in the project. This document defines both, states how they connect, places the works, and names who does what.

---

## Two entities

**Anonymous Art (AA) — the movement.**
A philosophy and an open body of work. Authorless, permissionless, owned by no one. Its charter is the [Manifesto](README.md): art without signatures, value revealed through interaction, the artist obsolete when the work succeeds. The movement makes and releases works, runs a digital-first school, and tours. Anyone may take part. It has no shareholders and no register.

**Anonymous Art Ltd (AA Ltd) — the company.**
A UK private company limited by shares, incorporated under the Companies Act 2006. It is at once a legal entity and an artwork — art using the company form, not a company pretending to be art. It issues twenty-one shares, holds assets, and offers a defined subset of works. Its charter is the [Articles of Association](ltd/ARTICLES.md), with the [Rules](ltd/RULES.md), [Rights](ltd/RIGHTS.md) and [Prospectus](ltd/PROSPECTUS.md).

**The relationship.**
The movement is the whole; the company is one instrument inside it. AA Ltd is itself a work of Anonymous Art — a legal and economic sculpture — and also the vehicle through which some works are held, issued, and funded. The movement can exist without the company; the company cannot exist outside the movement. Throughout the repository, "AA" means the movement; "AA Ltd" always means the company.

## The share structure in brief

AA Ltd issues **21 ordinary shares** — no more, absent a 75% supermajority. Each is partly paid (£1 of a £1,000,000 nominal value) and carries no promised return. Shares move through three editions on a **four-year cycle** — the same halving clock the whole project runs on:

- **A4 — Active:** original edition; full voting and participation; valid four years.
- **C4 — Continuation:** optional renewal every four years; retains voting.
- **R4 — Residual:** automatic if not renewed; ownership preserved, voting retired.

A retired certificate is not spent: the [Letter of Endorsement](ltd/ENDORSEMENT.md) makes its secondary-market value the shareholder's only return. The right decays on the clock; the object may appreciate. Full terms in the [Articles](ltd/ARTICLES.md).

## Roles

Participation runs at two levels — open, in the movement; defined, in the company.

### Movement — open to anyone

- **Creators** — make and release work anonymously. No permission, no attribution.
- **Audience / decoders** — complete the work by receiving it; meaning is activated in reception, not issued by the maker.
- **The school** — the digital-first teaching layer: courses, galleries, collective decoding.
- **Tippers** — send value straight to a work (value4value) over Lightning. Open to all, and the bridge into the company.

### Company — defined and finite

- **The Chosen — Shareholders (21).** The sovereign core. They own but do not operate: custodial, not executive. They appoint and remove the Director, amend the Articles, and decide reserved matters. Each share is itself a certificate-artwork. May be publicly anonymous. See [SHAREHOLDER.md](ltd/SHAREHOLDER.md).
- **The King — Director (1).** The sole executive and legal interface: statutory filings, compliance, custody of assets, execution of shareholder decisions. Holds the structure together; does not author the work. See [DIRECTOR.md](ltd/DIRECTOR.md).
- **The Witnesses — Non-Executive Directors (6).** One per domain: legal/IP, compliance, tax, technical, curatorial, communications. They advise, witness, and challenge; they hold no executive power and produce no works. They are living constraints that keep the project from collapsing into commerce or spectacle. See [NED.md](ltd/NED.md).
- **The Public / Tippers.** Visitors, participants, tippers. They cannot claim shares, but their attention and micro-payments are the system's fuel, and they are co-creators of its living economy. The Public is where the movement and the company meet.
- **The Treasury.** The company's node, held in a 2-of-3 multisig by three Treasury signatories — the Director and two of the Witnesses. AA Ltd's vault and broadcaster, and the organ that verifies its own holdings, moved by no single hand. See [TREASURY.md](ltd/TREASURY.md).

Authority runs one way, execution the other: **the Shareholders decide, the Director executes, the Witnesses advise, the Public fuels.** See [ECOSYSTEM.md](ltd/ECOSYSTEM.md).

## Involvement — the paths in

A ladder of deepening commitment, each rung voluntary:

1. **Encounter** — a QR code, in a gallery or online, opens the short Prospectus and the movement's ideas.
2. **Respond** — tip a work, decode it, attend a performance. Value4value, no commitment.
3. **Signal** — send one satoshi to register interest and receive the full [Prospectus](ltd/PROSPECTUS.md). The minimum possible signal.
4. **Participate** — accept the Invitation, pay to claim one of the 21 shares, and become one of the Chosen.

No rung is sold. The movement never markets; the company never convinces. Silence and declining are valid at every step. See the [Invite Funnel](ltd/INVITE_FUNNEL.md) and [Launch Roadmap](ltd/LAUNCH_ROADMAP.md).

## Anonymity, and its limit

Anonymity is real at three points: **authorship** (works carry no signature), **shareholding** (the Chosen may stay publicly anonymous), and **the council** (NEDs may serve pseudonymously).

It has one legal floor, stated plainly rather than implied away: as a UK company, AA Ltd must file its Director and any person of significant control with Companies House, on the public record, and must keep a register of members holding shareholders' true legal names. The register is private; the directorship is not. Public-facing anonymity is complete; legal anonymity is not.
