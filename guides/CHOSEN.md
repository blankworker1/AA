# THE CHOSEN

*A background guide: who the Chosen are, and who the Prospectus is for.*

The Chosen are not chosen. They choose for themselves.

This is a profile, not a marketing segment. The [Prospectus](PROSPECTUS.md) is itself a conceptual object, and who receives it is part of the work. What follows is a grounded account of who the Chosen are — and, just as importantly, who they are not. Their formal role and governance are defined in [SHAREHOLDER.md](../ltd/SHAREHOLDER.md); this is the thinking behind it.

---

## The Chosen

The shareholders, known as the Chosen, are sovereign individuals who understand what it means to decide. They recognise that freedom is not granted but exercised. Each has understood the vision and aims of Anonymous Art and made a conscious choice to participate.

They are collectors of assets. Art is an asset. Bitcoin is an asset. Shares are an asset. A limited-edition physical share certificate — legally recognised, partly paid in Bitcoin — is an apex asset, combining ownership, scarcity, legal force, and belief.

To hold one is not to speculate, but to belong. Ownership functions like a passport: quiet proof of participation in a system that operates on different rails.

The Chosen do not seek attention. They do not perform. They hold.

---

## Who the Prospectus is for

**1. Sovereign collectors.** People who already think in terms of ownership, custody, and self-direction — comfortable holding assets directly, clear on the difference between possession and control, not dependent on institutions to validate value. They don't ask "Is this allowed?" They ask "Does this make sense?"

**2. Asset-literate, not speculative.** Not chasing upside. They understand art as an asset class, Bitcoin as an asset rather than a trade, and shares as instruments of governance rather than leverage. They are comfortable with illiquidity, long time horizons, and assets that cannot be easily priced. If they ask "What's the exit plan?", they are not Chosen.

**3. Culturally bilingual.** They move between worlds without explanation — art and finance, law and culture, physical and digital. They understand that a legal document can be an artwork, a company can be a sculpture, and a share certificate can be more meaningful than a token. They don't need the metaphors explained.

**4. Low-signal, high-agency.** Rarely loud. Not obsessed with public profiles, not building personal brands, comfortable operating privately. They recognise that true power does not announce itself.

**5. Already inside the system, or already outside it.** Two archetypes: inside but disillusioned — senior figures in art, finance, law, or technology who understand the system's limits from within; or outside by design — independent thinkers, builders, collectors, or patrons who opted out early. Both understand that new structures must be built, not protested.

---

## Who the Prospectus is not for

Retail investors. Speculators. Influencers. Grant-seekers. People asking for permission. People who need reassurance.

If they need convincing, they are not Chosen.

---

## How the Prospectus is sent

Direct, private delivery. No public application, no pitch deck, no deadline pressure.

Receiving the Prospectus is not an offer; it is an invitation to decide. Silence is a valid response. Declining is respected. Choosing is the act.

---

## Final framing

The Prospectus is not about selling shares. It is about identifying

> people who already live as if this structure should exist.

Those who recognise it will recognise themselves.
